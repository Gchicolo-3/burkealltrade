# AllTrade Command: How To Use It

For Mike and anyone helping run the business. One task per page. Everything works from your phone.

The five buttons at the bottom of the screen: Today, Quote, Schedule, Jobs, People.

Nothing ever goes out on its own. The app writes the text, you tap to send it from your own phone.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the AllTrade link on your phone. Save it to your home screen so it works like an app.
2. Tap Sign in with Google.
3. Pick your Google account.
4. Say yes to Google Calendar so booked jobs show up on your calendar.

Only the accounts George set up can get in.

---

## 2. Today's follow ups

This is the home screen. It shows who to follow up with today: unpaid jobs, open quotes, thank yous after a job, and past customers you haven't talked to in a while.

1. Tap Today.
2. Read the text it wrote. Change anything you want right in the box.
3. Tap Text. Your messages app opens with the text ready. Hit send there.
4. Come back and tap Log touch so it comes off the list.

Don't like the wording? Tap Rewrite. Not this week? Tap Skip a week.

---

## 3. Quick quote

1. Tap Quote.
2. Pick the customer, or add a new one.
3. Under What needs doing, type it or tap the mic and talk. Add photos if you have them.
4. Tap Build quote. It breaks the job into lines with hours and materials.
5. Type your price on each line in Price $. You set the price, every job is its own.
6. Check the total, then tap Save and write it up.
7. Copy the text version or the email version and send it from your phone or email.

No time for the AI? Tap Skip AI, enter line items myself.

## 4. Schedule a job

1. Tap Schedule.
2. Pick the customer, or pick From an open quote.
3. Set the date, time, and how long.
4. Tap Book it.

It goes on your Google Calendar and writes a confirmation text. Tap Text to send it.

[SCREENSHOT: schedule form]

---

## 5. Mark done and paid

1. Tap Jobs.
2. On the Booked tab, find the job.
3. Tap Job done when the work is finished. It moves to Unpaid.
4. When the money comes in, tap Mark paid.

Got paid at the door? Tap Paid on the spot instead.

Unpaid jobs show up on Today with a friendly reminder text, so nobody slips through.

[SCREENSHOT: Jobs screen with tabs]

---

## 6. Bring in your customers

1. Tap People, then Import customers.
2. Pick your file: a spreadsheet saved as CSV, or contact cards (.vcf) from your phone.
3. Check the list. Untick anyone you don't want.
4. Tap Import.

Duplicates get skipped. To add one person by hand, tap Add customer.

---

## Settings (once)

- Your voice: paste 2 or 3 real texts you've sent customers. The drafts copy how you write.
- Google review link: goes into the thank you texts after a paid job.
- Pricing: the hourly rate is optional. Leave it blank and you price every line yourself.
